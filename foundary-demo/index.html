THE PROBLEM

People should handle exceptions. Not re-read every page.

Healthcare administrative work often begins with documents that have to be opened, interpreted, re-keyed, compared, checked for completeness, and routed. Foundary is designed to organize that work before staff spends time on the next system.

⇩

Receive the packet

Start with the documents the team already receives instead of requiring a replacement practice system.

⌕

Extract the facts

Convert unstructured pages into configured fields that can be reviewed and used downstream.

≡

Compare records

Check repeated information across supplied documents so discrepancies are visible together.

✓

Escalate exceptions

Keep judgment with people. Foundary surfaces what needs attention rather than silently guessing.

HOW IT WORKS

One packet. One structured path.

The system sits around an existing healthcare workflow. It does not require a client to replace an EHR, practice-management system, or payer portal just to begin removing repetitive document work.

01

Packet enters

A staff member uploads the packet today. Other authorized intake channels can be connected later.

02

Documents are understood

Foundary identifies document content and extracts configured administrative fields.

03

Records are compared

The system checks for configured missing, inconsistent, or conflicting information.

04

Routine work is organized

Clean information is assembled into a consistent review record instead of being re-keyed across documents.

05

Exceptions go to people

Anything requiring judgment or correction is routed for human review rather than guessed.

06

Work continues in existing systems

Staff uses the review result to complete the next authorized step in their existing software.
